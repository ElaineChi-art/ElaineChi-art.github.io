Crafted Without Compromise

Nothing here is decorative.

Every element of the Perpetual 40 exists because it solves a problem in mechanical timekeeping. What looks like ornament is tolerance, and what looks like restraint is engineering.

Perpetual Calibre

A self-winding rotor converts the smallest movement of the wrist into stored energy. Worn daily, the watch never stops and never needs setting.

18ct Rose Gold

Cast in our own foundry from a proprietary alloy of gold, copper and platinum. The platinum fraction holds the warmth of the rose against decades of light.

Hermetic Case

A middle case machined from a solid block, a screw-down crown, and a caseback torqued by a dedicated key. Sealed to one hundred metres.

Cascade Bracelet

Semi-circular three-piece links, each finished by hand and articulated on a concealed pin. The folding clasp disappears entirely into the profile.

Chronometer Certified

Every movement is tested independently, then re-tested cased. We publish a tolerance of minus two to plus two seconds per day — twice as strict as the standard.

Magnified Date

A sapphire lens ground to two and a half times magnification, fused to the crystal above the aperture, with an anti-reflective coating on the underside only.
