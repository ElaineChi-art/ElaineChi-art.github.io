Built To Be Opened

Every part comes apart.

Headphones fail at the pads, the cable and the headband — in that order. All three are user-replaceable here, without tools, and will be stocked for at least fifteen years.

Beryllium Dome

A 50 mm diaphragm vapour-deposited in a single piece. Beryllium is stiff enough to stay pistonic past 25 kHz, which is why the top end never turns brittle.

Milled Baffle

Cut from a solid aluminium billet over forty minutes, then hard-anodised. A cast baffle would ring; this one is dead below the audible floor.

Tuned Leak

Six acoustic vents behind the driver, each a different diameter. They are what turn a sealed box into a bass response that stays flat to 18 Hz.

Lambskin Pads

Perforated on the inner wall only, so the seal stays airtight where it matters. Twist and lift to replace — no adhesive, no clips to snap.

Sprung Headband

A spring-steel band under a leather sleeve carries the weight; the yokes only steer. Clamping force is 3.4 newtons and does not change as the leather ages.
